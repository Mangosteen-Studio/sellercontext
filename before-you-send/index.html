    How the checker works
    Rules v1.2.0 use fixed English-language patterns, not an AI model. It checks filler, vague phrases, sentences over 30 words, paragraphs over 80 words, repeated three-word phrases, three or more repeated clause openings, common “not X, it’s Y” frames, stock transitions, and language that may need evidence. There is no overall word limit recommendation or personal style preset to configure.
    The same text and rules version produce the same results. Quotes, abbreviations, deliberate rhetoric, and technical writing can trigger unhelpful suggestions. These are email-originated heuristics, not a validated assessment of LinkedIn performance. We do not verify facts, infer authorship, or automatically rewrite your work. Reloading or clearing removes your draft from this session.
