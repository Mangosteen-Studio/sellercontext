Worked example / DeadAccount

From sales context to a useful story.

An illustrative, editorially prepared concept one-pager—not a measured AI comparison or a claim of current product availability. It uses the public DeadAccount founder profile and its value map: a cleaner book, less territory research, and a more deliberate week.

DeadAccountILLUSTRATIVE CONCEPT · REVIEW DRAFT

Know which accounts deserve your week.

For account executives with a named-account book and limited time to investigate it.

The territory changes. Your list may not.

An inherited book can contain companies that have closed, changed ownership, or stopped fitting what you sell. Working through that list takes attention away from the accounts worth a closer look.

The proposed workflow

DeadAccount is designed to investigate the book you already own and return an explained order of attention. The intended handoff connects company evidence to your selling context, so you can inspect the reasoning before deciding where to spend your time.

The value is a clearer weekly plan. You keep the final judgment about whom to pursue and what to say.

Evaluate the work, not a revenue promise.

A useful evaluation would start with accounts you know. Check whether the evidence supports each recommendation, whether uncertainty is visible, and which decisions you would change. No customer outcomes or measured time savings are claimed in this example.

Start with the part of your book you distrust.

Which accounts would you want to check first—and what would make the result useful enough to act on?

What the SELLER.md changed

  • Audience: the AE working an existing book, not every sales persona at once.
  • Value map: a clearer allocation of selling time, rather than a list of AI features.
  • Judgment: inspect the evidence and keep the seller's decision in charge.
  • Claims: no invented ROI, customer logos, pricing, availability, or pipeline promises.
  • Next step: a concrete question, not an automatic demo request.

Before customer use: confirm current capability, add approved proof if available, and approve the final wording. BestPatch's new-territory offer is deliberately not folded into DeadAccount's existing-book story.
