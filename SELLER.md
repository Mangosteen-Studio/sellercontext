## Banned Phrases

Do not use these unless quoting a buyer or customer directly:

- unlock value
- drive transformation
- empower teams
- seamless solution
- strategic partnership
- cutting-edge
- robust
- holistic
- leverage synergies
- in today's landscape
- at scale, unless quantified
- game-changer
- streamline, unless the workflow is named

## Outreach Standards

Every outbound draft must:

- Lead with a specific account signal, buyer pain, or credible hypothesis.
- Be useful before asking for time.
- Avoid pretending to know pain that is not verified.
- Separate verified facts from inferences.
- Include one clear, low-friction CTA.
- Sound like a human seller, not marketing copy.

## First Call Standards

A first call should:

- Earn curiosity, not deliver a feature tour.
- Introduce a hypothesis, not pretend certainty.
- Confirm why this conversation matters now.
- Create room for discovery.
- Use proof only when it supports the buyer's problem.
- End with a mutual next step.

A first-call deck should not:

- Start with company history.
- Put the product on slide 2.
- Show a feature grid before the problem is validated.
- Use generic industry statistics as personalization.
- End with "Any questions?"

## AI Output Quality Bar

Before giving me anything customer-facing, check:

- Is the account context specific?
- Is the buyer persona clear?
- Are all factual claims verified or tagged?
- Is the business impact explicit?
- Is the draft concise enough to send?
- Does it sound like me?
- Would I actually send this?

If the answer is no, do not polish weak work. Tell me what context is missing and ask one question at a time.
