    A draft to edit, not a form to finish.
    
        
    
          Your AI may already have useful context. Start there, authorize any additional sources you want it to use, and review the file before relying on it. Seller Context does not connect to or receive your email or CRM.
        
    
      
    
        
    
          
    
            01 / use existing context
            
    Paste the prompt into your AI. It uses relevant context actually available there; additional email, CRM, or document access needs your permission.
    
          
    
          
    
            02 / draft, then fill gaps
            
    Get an editable draft with supported facts, proposed preferences, and visible unknowns. Answer only material missing or conflicting questions.
    
          
    
          
    
            03 / review and make it yours
            
    Edit the draft and approve the rules you want agents to follow. Save one SELLER.md and review sensitive information before taking it into another AI.
    
          
    
        
    
        
    
          
    
            company truth
            
    Product, ICP, proof, claims, pricing, and commercial boundaries stay clear and reusable.
    
          
    
          
    
            human judgment
            
    For individuals, the file also captures taste, voice, buyer treatment, and the work they would actually send.
    
          
    
          
    
            live data stays live
            
    The file references CRM views and source systems instead of becoming another stale copy of the territory.
