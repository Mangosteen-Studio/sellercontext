Question 1 of 14About 7 minutes

            

          


          

          

            

              Start with you
              
              
              Use the name you want to appear in your generated seller context.
            

            

              Your role
              
              
              Be specific enough for the agent to understand your responsibilities and motion.
            

            

              Your company
              
              
              This anchors the product, proof, policies, and competitive context.
            

            

              Your territory
              
              
              Include segment, region, vertical, or named-account scope.
            

            

              Your account universe
              
              
              Reference the CRM view or CSV that holds the full list. Keep only priority accounts and the territory logic in SELLER.md.
            

            

              Your offer
              
              
              Explain it like you would to a smart new teammate—not like a homepage.
            

            

              Best fit
              
              
              The sharper this is, the better your agents can prioritize accounts.
            

            

              The buying group
              
              
              Use real titles and the language each persona responds to.
            

            

              Credibility
              
              
              Only include proof you can verify. Your agent should never invent a metric.
            

            

              Your edge
              
              
              Honest constraints produce better positioning than a perfect-sounding answer.
            

            

              Sound like you
              
              
              Describe what you would actually send and how care should show up in the work—not what corporate copy sounds like.
            

            

              Your judgment
              
              
              Good judgment includes knowing when not to optimize for the next meeting.
            

            

              Buyer resistance
              
              
              Exact buyer language is much more useful than generic objection labels.
            

            

              Your taste
              
              
              Examples reveal your actual taste more clearly than a list of adjectives.
